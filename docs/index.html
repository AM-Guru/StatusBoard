Status Board

A live dashboard for everything you keep an eye on — on your Mac, iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, and Apple Watch.

What it does

  • Build dashboards from 24 kinds of panel: clocks, weather, graphs, progress bars, RSS feeds, calendars, countdowns, tables, service status, images, and free text.
  • Web clips — pick a region of any web page and show just that region, with content blocking and scheduled refresh.
  • Connectors for GitHub, App Store Connect, Supabase (including SQL), web server logs and analytics, Apple Health, and MCP tools.
  • School panels for Canvas / Instructure and K12 OLS: grades, today's schedule, and assignments due, late, or needing a re-do.
  • A bridge on your Mac that pushes values from scripts and command-line tools straight onto every screen, over your local network.
  • Widgets on the Home Screen, Lock Screen, desktop, and watch face, plus Live Activities, Control Center, Shortcuts, and Spotlight.
  • iCloud sync of your dashboards between your own devices.

Privacy

No accounts. No analytics. No tracking. No developer server of any kind. Read the privacy policy.
